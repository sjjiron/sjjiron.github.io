About Us

A full-range steel supplier, built on trust.

SJJ Iron & Steels supplies the complete range of iron and steel products, from raw material to finished sections, for re-rolling mills, fabricators, EPC contractors and stockists across India. We carry particular specialisation in sponge iron (DRI), alongside billets, TMT bars, structural steel, coils and plates.
